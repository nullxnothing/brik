# BRIK Subscription, Metering & Profitability

## Anonymous trial — $0, no account
Purpose: the magic moment before signup.

Ephemeral sandbox with minutes-long TTL; one guided template; devnet only; locked-down egress; no secrets; rate-limited per IP/fingerprint; hard per-session compute and AI cap. Signup converts the sandbox into a saved project. Perceived generosity stays high; real exposure stays bounded and priced per session.

## Free — $0
Purpose: acquisition.

Public projects; limited private trial; shared compute; devnet; templates; limited AI; limited workspace hours/storage; GitHub import/export; aggressive hibernation. Shareable devnet preview URLs stay free — they are the growth loop, not a paywall lever.

**No unlimited AI or compute.** Gating beyond the trial may require a credit card or minimum GitHub account age (abuse control, not monetization).

## Hackathon credits
Time-boxed credit grants for Colosseum/Foundation/university events, provisioned per event with expiry and a conversion offer at event end. Track cost per hackathon cohort and conversion separately from organic free users.

## Builder — $25/month
Private projects; persistent workspaces; larger compute/AI allowance; mainnet tooling; more storage; secrets; production deployment access; BYOK when supported.

## Pro — $79/month + usage
Stronger workspaces; multiple production apps; staging/production environments; logs; monitoring; background workers where supported; higher allowances; priority builds.

## Team — from $199/month + usage
Multiple seats; shared projects; roles/permissions; deployment approvals; shared secrets/environments; audit history; pooled usage; priority support.

Avoid unnecessary per-seat tax if usage is the main value driver.

## Scale — custom
High quotas; dedicated/private runners where required; private networking; SSO/compliance when demanded; SLA/support.

## Metering
- normalized AI credits;
- workspace CPU/RAM hours;
- build minutes;
- persistent storage;
- production runtime;
- log retention;
- premium environments.

## Natural upgrade triggers
Persistence, private work, exhausted AI, mainnet, production hosting, teammates, monitoring, higher compute.

**Do not paywall the first successful devnet experience.**

## Gross margin
Long-term planning target: **70%+ blended gross margin**. Track subscription, AI, workspace, and production infrastructure margins separately.

## Free-user budget
Until real data exists, use **$2-$5/month per activated free user** only as a planning ceiling, plus a separate per-session ceiling for anonymous trials (cents, not dollars — enforced by TTL and caps). Enforce hibernation, quotas, routing, caching, storage limits, rate limits, and anti-abuse controls. Crypto tooling plus free compute attracts miners, drainer builders, and bots — abuse controls ship together with every increase in free exposure, never after.

## Example validation cohort
- 200 Builder × $25 = $5,000
- 50 Pro × $79 = $3,950
- 10 Team × $250 average = $2,500

**Subscription MRR = $11,450**, before usage.

The real test is retention and gross margin after AI/compute COGS.

## Annual billing
Add only after retention is understood. A 15-20% effective discount is reasonable later. Never use annual prepayment to disguise churn.
